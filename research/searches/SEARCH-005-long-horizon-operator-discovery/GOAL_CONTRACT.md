# SEARCH-005 goal contract

## Immutable objective

Discover, derive, implement and locally validate a new clean-UNSB mathematical
update that can retain beneficial behavior over long training. The candidate
must arise from observed reversal evidence and must modify an update operator,
gradient estimator, bridge coordinate or coupled training dynamics.

## In scope

- causal decomposition of correction-field validity and downstream propagation;
- evidence-routed construction of at most three minimal algorithms;
- continuous constrained operators, unbiased estimators, reparameterizations and
  coupled state dynamics;
- identity, self-null, unbiasedness or stability properties derived before runs;
- paired discovery metrics used only after a branch/run to evaluate scientific
  outcome.

## Out of scope

- fixed activation or withdrawal windows;
- threshold searches and hyperparameter grids;
- annealing whose only purpose is to postpone the same failure;
- online paired PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS control;
- choosing and committing an entire plain or proposal state branch;
- output imitation of plain UNSB;
- gap-aware handoff or optimizer-state repair after a preselected exit.

Continuous state dependence is not automatically forbidden. It is admissible
only when it follows from a coherent operator, estimator or constrained problem
and changes the correction geometrically; an on/off selector does not qualify.

## Mandatory candidate record

Before implementation, every candidate must state:

1. parent probe and causal evidence;
2. the UNSB mathematical object being repaired;
3. the complete update or estimator formula;
4. identity, self-null, unbiasedness or stability property;
5. why the construction remains meaningful after the early positive window;
6. proof that paired targets are inaccessible to training;
7. a fast falsification experiment;
8. allowed and forbidden scientific claims;
9. compute and memory cost.

## Claim boundary

Route 1 may be declared exhausted only when every failure class supported by the
causal audit has produced an admissible minimal construction, each construction
has received its permitted evidence-driven revision, and no supported failure
class remains untested. A failed controller, schedule or handoff cannot close
this route.
