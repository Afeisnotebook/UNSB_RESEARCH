# SEARCH-004 route-2 protocol

## Question hierarchy

1. **Is a positive state naturally inheritable?** Compare uninterrupted,
   complete-state hard disable, and common-clock plain.
2. **Where is benefit stored?** Ablate G, G/F, G/F+moments, D/E/F equilibration,
   and optimizer tangent state.
3. **Is there a measured transition defect?** A new transport is admitted only
   when it reduces a target-blind defect and is not identity.
4. **Does defect repair improve continuation?** Compare at 32/200 and then 800
   updates.  Paired metrics are computed only after each branch.
5. **Does it last?** Continue the surviving source/transport with matched plain
   and hard-disable controls to total step 3200 on small25, evaluating
   discovery70 every 400 continuation steps.

## Generation-0 causal arms

- `P_common_plain`: matched plain parameters, common future clock.
- `U_uninterrupted`: the source intervention remains active.
- `A_hard_disable`: complete method state, native objective/update immediately.
- `B_gf_zero_moment`: delete inherited G/F Adam history.
- `C_local_native_moment`: replace G/F moments with shadow native accumulation.
- `D0_hold_only`: hold G without co-state updates.
- `D_costate_equilibration`: hold G while D/E/F take native updates.
- `E_combined`: native moment reconstruction plus co-state equilibration.
- `F_g_only_transplant`: method G on the plain component control.
- `G_gf_transplant`: method G/F parameters on the plain component control.
- `K_gf_state_transplant`: method G/F parameters and Adam states on the plain
  D/E component control.

The transplant arms are causal probes.  Using a matched plain component is not
allowed to become an output-imitation loss or an online reference.

## Generation-1 transport

`H_native_moment_projection` implements G1-LCNMP, the least-change projection
of inherited effective Adam moments onto current unpaired native-descent
half-spaces.  It must reduce its own native constraint violation; an identity
result is classified as "no optimizer tangent defect" and cannot be promoted
as an algorithmic gain.

A co-state transport is generated only if equilibration improves over both
hard disable and hold-only on a positive source.  The fixed 32-step ablation is
not itself a candidate.

## Gates

Promotion from 200 to 800 requires target-blind defect reduction (or an exact
component-carrier attribution), matched lift over hard disable, trajectory AUC,
domain coverage and worst-domain protection.  The long continuation additionally
requires positive late-three and final deltas, at least 4/6 final positive
domains, worst domain above -1 dB, non-degraded SSIM/LPIPS, and no collapse of
the candidate's absolute PSNR relative to its source state.

`confirmation20` remains sealed throughout.
